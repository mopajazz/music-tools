Music Tools

Small browser instruments for playing, practicing and writing music. Nothing to install.

Play

  • Grand

    A concert piano in your browser, with a metronome.

    /grand/

  • MIDI Bloom Beta

    Fly a world that blooms as you play. Works with a MIDI keyboard, the computer keys, a webcam or a mic.

    /midi-bloom/

Practice

  • Chord Loop

    Loop the changes around the cycle of fourths or chromatically, and play along.

    jazzinteractive.org ↗

  • Jazz Ear Trainer

    Train your ear on chord tones and altered tensions over a ii–V–I.

    /ear-trainer/

Write

  • Syncopate

    An ABC notation lab for jazz: type a tune, see the score, hear it back.

    /syncopate/
